    How IGF-1 LR3 Works in Ann Arbor

    Natural IGF-1 is a 70 amino acid hormone made mostly by the liver, and it lasts only minutes in the blood before binding proteins grab it. The LR3 version has a 13 amino acid extension and an arginine swapped in at position 3. Those changes keep it away from binding proteins, so it stays active for many hours. Longer action, more risk. Human research on LR3 specifically is very limited, and it is not FDA-approved.

    Who Usually Asks About IGF-1 LR3

    Typical candidates are healthy adults who train 3 or more days a week, eat enough protein and want supervised support for recovery or lean mass. Many have already tried a growth hormone secretagogue. Competitive athletes should stop here. IGF-1 and its analogs are banned under WADA rules.

    Side Effects and Safety in Ann Arbor

    Low blood sugar is the main short-term danger, showing up as shakiness, sweating, confusion or a racing heart, and it can come on fast if you dosed on an empty stomach. Joint pain, swelling and headache are also reported. Then there is cancer. IGF-1 signaling encourages cell growth, so any existing tumor is a concern, and long-term safety in healthy adults is unknown.

    CJC-1295 with Ipamorelin, Sermorelin and Tesamorelin all prompt your pituitary to release its own growth hormone, which then raises IGF-1 while your body's feedback loop stays in charge.
